the packs below. **Nothing here is claimed CC0 unless its source page says so.**

and filename; audition before wiring into the game (Phase 2).

Game credits use the pseudonym **Aethelwarden Solas** for James's own work.
That does NOT replace the third-party attributions below: the CC-BY 3.0
items legally require their named credit lines, which must appear in the
game's credits in addition to the pseudonym.

## Deliberately NOT downloaded (with reasons)

- **BBC Sound Effects (RemArc licence): NOT downloaded.** Its terms,
  exactly as published, limit use to **personal, educational, or
  research projects only**; commercial use requires a separate licence.
  A distributable game — even a free one — is not covered by the free
  RemArc licence, so no BBC file is staged here and none may be
  relabelled as CC0 or free-use.
- **Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles: NOT downloaded.** Licence verified
  as royalty-free and commercially usable, no attribution required,
  unlimited projects for life, media-production (games/film/TV/
  interactive) use only, no AI/ML training, no standalone
  redistribution of the sounds themselves
  (https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/). The 2026 bundle alone is
  7.47 GB / 347 files — far too large to cherry-pick blindly, and no
  per-file list was available to target creature vocals specifically.
  A future pass could download it once and extract only
  creature-vocal files, if the size budget allows.
- **Freesound: NOT used.** Account required to download — excluded by
  the task rules.
- **itch.io: NOT used.** Excluded by the task rules; no workaround
  attempted (this also blocks Kenney's itch-hosted all-in-one bundle
  and the Kenney Voiceover Pack (Fighter) direct download — the
  older Voiceover Pack above was used instead via OpenGameArt).
- **Kenney audio already in-project (NOT re-downloaded):**
  `assets/audio/kenney/` already holds Impact Sounds, Interface
  Sounds, Music Jingles, and RPG Audio (all CC0, License.txt in each
  folder), covering attack cues and footsteps for the 28 staged
  monsters. Nothing from those packs was duplicated here.
- **rubberduck "80 CC0 creature SFX" (pack #1):** CC0 1.0,
  confirmed Nov 4 via OGA credits record. All 80 files staged Nov 7
  as rd1-*.ogg; Bucket A (58) wired the same day per James's
  green-light; Buckets B (13), C (6), D (3) staged-only, held.
  The sibling "80 CC0 RPG SFX" pack was not downloaded (no direct
  file URL in readable page text; guessed URLs 404).
- **Within downloaded packs, excluded as non-creature audio:**
  WAV duplicates of every Little Robot MP3; Fantasy ambience,
  jingles, gold pickups, menu/inventory sounds; Horror ambience,
  jingles, gate, evil-machine loop, blood drips; Kenney announcer
  lines. Exclusion is a curation choice, not a licence problem.
- **No ripped audio.** Nothing here comes from any game, anime, or
  film. Nothing with no stated licence was downloaded.

- **Kappa water-gurgle sample (Nov 4, James-approved):** shaped with
  ffmpeg from OGA pack #1 burble_01.ogg + burble_02.ogg (rubberduck,
