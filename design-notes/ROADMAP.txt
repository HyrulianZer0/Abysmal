================================================================
ABYSMAL — Project Roadmap
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas
Status as of: November 1, 2026 | Current build: v0.11.0 (Android) + Windows demo v5 (+ Tristan playtest package, same build)
Next push: PENDING the director's green light (Nov 1: winding up, not ready yet) — one push per week
================================================================

WHAT IT IS: a 2.5D first-person idle dungeon-crawler roguelike for
Android (PC demos for testing). The thesis: "the endless abyss is the
endgame, with no end." No timelimit, no pressure, non-canon quests,
infinite growth. You delve, the dungeon plays itself, you steer it.

HOW WE WORK (the pacing method): at most ONE development push per
week, by design. It keeps the work sustainable, the feedback loop
clean, and every build worth testing properly. Versioning is not
decimal (after 0.10.0 comes 0.11.0); 1.0 is reserved for public launch.
Work is staged inside a push — fixes first, then NPCs, then the Life
Tree start — so no week ever blows past its budget blind.

Status key: [LIVE] in v0.10.0 | [0.11.0] shipped Oct 24 | [STAGED] in the project, not in any build | [PLAN] designed, parked | [OPEN] still to decide

       brick + moss), wall caps + brick headers above doors — no more
       black void. Door rebuild: full double-door textures on proper
       quad faces, visible red shackle on locked doors, readable LOCKED
       labels on both faces.
[0.11.0] NPC EMBODIMENT: the breadcrumb five stand in the dungeon at
       depths 2/4/6/8/10 — Marla Bramble (farmer), Harlan Slate (miner),
       Tovan Ashe (woodcutter), Sarella Vex (blacksmith), Pello Quin
       (trader). Their passives root into the Life Tree automatically.
       Silhouettes OK as placeholders; Quaternius-style CC0 models
       preferred. Depth-10 tavern scene: Pello sells you the deed for
       gold; signing opens the TOWN BUILDING MANAGEMENT menu.
[0.11.0] LIFE TREE START: the persistent infinite skill tree, first
       stage. Unlocks on completing depth 1. Opens with the 5 founders
       + helpful stat buffs. Web, not branches: no gates, every node
       always available (currency-gated only); no bad picks, so no
       respec is ever needed. Break-null lives here: NO stat caps, ever
       (crit past 100% always lands, overflow adds crit DAMAGE).
       The tree is the soul made visible — a LAUREL (leafed crown),
       not rings: the soul earns 6 rings (3 left, 3 right) and trades
       them in for the laurel, one-time and permanent. Tap a leaf for
       its stats panel and its vein-tree of buffs. The VINE binding the
       leaves is the ascension tree itself — an ouroboros circle,
       never overgrowing, never shrinking. Navigation is center-first:
       the vine is home; leaves branch off it; a laurel-shaped HOME
       button always returns you to the crown.

DECIDED — Nov 1, 2026 (locked by the director)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Texture pack: one house resource pack, 1K realistic anchor (see
  STAGED textures above); expansion ladder if wanted: 1K
  high/extreme, 512 high/medium, 256 medium/low, 128 low.
- Mercy: each mercy trigger shaves 10% off the foe's CURRENT HP
  (compounding, never zero), until the player defeats THAT foe;
  attack untouched; next spawn of the type starts fresh. Scope:
  individual foe, not type, not floor (it could be an apex or one
  boosted skeleton).
- Traps: disarming a trapped chest (or trapped door — doors are an
  option too) is a d20 roll: 1 = critical fail (most to all of the
  loot lost), 20 = critical success (bonus loot + learn the trap),
  2-19 graduate between. Mechanical chest traps are ADDED alongside
  mimics (mimics are the chest-monster surprise; chest traps are
  the disarmable kind).
- Zones: monster placement is RANDOM, not coded per zone — no
  zone-exclusive list. The game isn't resource farming; it's endless
  enjoyment with no endgame. (Theme structure already random.)
- Audio: jalastram-voice-008.mp3 (16.3 s gasp) REPURPOSED as a
  zombie/ghoul gasping loop, undead ambient — not cut. Pool move
  from FLAGGED lands in the next push.
- Itch.io clickthrough APPROVED if the assets are free and want
  credit (Quaternius Animated Easy Enemies, KayKit, LOWPO goblins).
- The laurel IS the Life Tree's form (vine = ascension tree, leaves
  = skills/buffs, upgrades indefinitely); the standalone Life Tree
  design paper is folded into the laurel draft.
- Town is a clipboard management interface (Oct 11): tap a building,
  its menu loads; tap the clipboard, jump destinations. Tavern and
  guild upgrades live in the clipboard too.
- SEQUENCING (Nov 1): finish the abyss entirely BEFORE tavern/town
  work. Jeff, Tristan, and Fen playtest the endless
  dungeon-delving loop meanwhile; town fine-tuning (city, tavern,
  buildings, more buildings, maybe more towns) comes after — ideas,
  not locked.

PLANNED — designed, build order flexible
----------------------------------------------------------------
[PLAN] HOPE currency (replaces platinum, Oct 24): the town's strength
       raises the people's Hope. Buildings are the Hope ENGINE
       (upgrades raise Hope income); the Life Tree is the Hope SINK
       (Hope -> permanent power). Sequencing, not starvation.
       Currency ladder: gold (mortal) -> Hope (prestige) -> Ambrosia
       (divine). Compounding: single clean multiplier M = 2^(p+a) on
       whole-tree power. Cost scaling ~10^N per loop.
[PLAN] PRESTIGE defined: prestige = BUILDING THE TOWN and receiving its
       buffs — both PERMANENT buffs (soul-level, survive rebirth) and
       RUN-BASED buffs (current life only, re-earned each life).
[PLAN] REBIRTH + AMBROSIA: sacrifice reframed as rebirth — gain Ambrosia
       for ascension through being remade, not through loss. Only the
       player character resets (life/delve/gear); town, Life Tree, Hope,
       and everyone in it persist — and that persistence makes the
       rewards greater (town strength feeds rebirth yield). The Life
       Tree persists through rebirth because it IS your soul. Ambrosia
       is spent at the Temple. (The old invisible Abyssal Token retires
       into this economy; any award gets a real moment: banner + sound
       + visible counter.)
[PLAN] TOWN BUILDINGS: built one by one, each with LEVELS that raise its
       stat bonuses and unlock more of its options. Build order is the
       PLAYER'S CHOICE — all buildings visible but GREYED OUT until
       requirements are met. Each building needs its RELEVANT RESOURCES
       (gathering-zone materials, not just gold). Town unlocks at depth
       10 via the tavern. Guild Hall is the dispatch center — leveling
       it grows dispatch slots. Roster: Tavern (food/regen buffs,
       adventurer pool), Smith (forge gear, upgrade adventurers' gear),
       Alchemist (potions), Guild Hall (delving skills: lockpicking,
       perception, stealth), Temple (luck/rarity + Ambrosia tree),
       Training Grounds (core stats), Mage's Guild (below), Life Tree.
[PLAN] MAGE'S GUILD (the Arcanum): home of spells and enchanting.
       Adventurers AUTO-CAST their spells. Player enchantments are
       PERMANENT boosts — no recharge costs; improved enchantments are
       just stronger. Progression is not strictly solo-first: level the
       Arcanum early for adventurer spells; a GUILD MASTER is required
       for a specific task/floor later, by which point the player has
       unlocked some enchantments.
[PLAN] GATHERING ZONES: Forest (wood), Caverns (stone/metal), Swamp
       (herbs) — BOTH guild-dispatch destinations AND places you can
       delve yourself. New dungeon themes/textures + zone-appropriate
       monsters. Zones feed town construction.
[PLAN] MONSTER RESOURCE DROPS: monsters drop thematic resources — wolf
       -> fur, boar -> leather, spider -> silk. More monsters = more
       resource variety; drops feed crafting and building. "Use the
       monster to the advantage of the player."
[PLAN] Guild dispatch: timed missions, 4 classes, composition puzzle,
       units can fail but always return with something. NO permadeath.
[PLAN] Undercrucible (the Converter): melt gear into DROSSGLASS to
       strengthen existing gear (materials -> existing items). Blacksmith
       creates NEW gear from gold (craft base -> enhance loop). Full
       extraction: no drop-only tier — endless depth outscales any weapon.
[PLAN] Delve Endlessly: one continuous run, no floor saved, death ends
       it. The endless abyss as endgame. (Replaces the old hardcore idea.)
[PLAN] Elements: damage types AND interactions (fire melts ice, etc.);
       elements live in the Charm slot.
[PLAN] Forge Houses (approved): weapon behavior identities — Keenbrand
       (crits bleed), Marrowdeep (hits heal), Anvilborn (slow/huge),
       Vexwing (every swing hits 3x), Mirrorhold (riposte), Knell
       (executes the wounded).
[PLAN] Mimic + Tsukumogami chests: some chests are monsters. Win = double
       loot and/or better quality. Mimics (common) breathe faintly;
       Tsukumogami (rare mini-boss, a century-old chest) are pristine and
       ornate — the richness is the warning. Perception can spot them.
[PLAN] Monster roster: ~300 public-domain folklore bases across 28
       families + element attunements, corruption forms, size variants =
       ~1,000 roster entries. IP-clean by design (names/ideas aren't
       copyrighted; only specific designs are avoided).
[PLAN] Accessibility: Windows Magnifier compatibility REQUIRED
       (Nov 1, from Tristan himself) + large-text/UI-scale option —
       required before Tristan (legally blind) can test or play.
       Tristan playtest package prepared Nov 1 (v0.11.0 Windows demo,
       same build — predates the accessibility pass; his feedback
       shapes it).
[PLAN] Later: named uniques (11 on paper, unique-as-rarity-roll), the
       full 39-rung rarity ladder in-game, town clipboard UI polish,
       blacksmith crafting UI, monster behaviors (swarms, kiters,
       chargers...) per family, slow-mo dev cheat (0.5x/0.25x/0.1x),
       hand polish (smoother hand, sharper blade, finer guard; distinct
       slash vs jab; smoother walk; kick-open chests).

KEY DECISIONS (locked in by the director)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- No caps, ever: skill, prestige, ascension, stats — infinite growth.
- Generosity rule: the worst run still moves five needles.
- Offline never paywalled; busy lives respected.
- Original names/lore/art/UI only — no IP borrowed from other games.
- Chests: fight first, double-check second, then open. Hidden traps get
  a faint visual hint first.
- Town is a clipboard management interface: tap a building, you're in;
  tap the clipboard, you're somewhere else. No walking commute.
- The dungeon core (delve/loot/die/grow) is BUILT and self-sustaining;
  everything from here is expansion.
- The fantasy: "be the change you want to see in the world" — improving
  yourself AND the world. Rebirth is transformation, not loss.
