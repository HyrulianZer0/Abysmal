## Target profile (content guidance)

A true harpy screech = a hawk/eagle-like raptor cry, fierce and bird-like.
NOT a human scream, NOT a ghost wail (wraith-like wails already rejected),
NOT a generic monster roar, NOT a trimmed ambience, NOT a pitched
variation of a generic sound.

Useful content fact found this pass (multiple independent sources):
the iconic fierce "eagle scream" of film is in fact a RED-TAILED HAWK cry —
a hoarse, descending "kee-eeeee-arr," usually 2–3 seconds. Real bald-eagle
calls are weak chirps/whistles, NOT a fierce scream. So red-tailed hawk
screeches are the RIGHT target profile for a harpy, and true bald-eagle
chirps are the WRONG profile. (Sources: birdshow.blog, reptileknowledge,
IFLScience/Cornell Lab, NPS — all agree.)

## License standard used here (same as batch 1/2)

- VERIFIED means the license text was seen ON the source page itself in
  this pass (direct page fetch). A third-party list claiming a license is
  NOT enough — third-party claims are labeled as such and never upgraded
  to verified.
- CC0 preferred; CC-BY acceptable with credit. Custom free in-game-embed
  licenses acceptable only if their terms are seen on-page.
- NOT shippable, unchanged: SFXMint (terms unresolved/conflicting),
  SoundFactory, Violet Recording (prototyping-only per ToS), BBC, Meta
  AudioCraft, Zapsplat-free. Account-gated sources are out for this hunt
  by definition (Freesound, Uppbeat). Paid sources are out (Soundstripe,
  BOOM Library, Hollywood Edge / Sound Ideas libraries, Sonniss bundles
  — GB-scale, deprioritized in batch 1). CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-SA and GFDL files
  are NOT in the approved license list and are not recommended here.
