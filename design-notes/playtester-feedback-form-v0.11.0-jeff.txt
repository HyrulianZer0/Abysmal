Jeff — thank you. Your design feedback (Oct 20) already reshaped this
game: Hope, the Life Tree, the laurel itself. This round is NOT design
feedback. This round is your GAMEPLAY-FEEL eyes: how it feels to play
on Windows, moment to moment. That's the view we still owe the game,
and it's the one only you can give us right now.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR ON THIS BUILD:
- Ceilings and doors: do the new ceiling slabs and rebuilt double
  doors read clearly? Do locked doors (red shackle, LOCKED labels)
  read instantly, or do you have to squint?
- The founders: Marla, Harlan, Tovan, Sarella, and Pello standing in
  the dungeon at depths 2/4/6/8/10 — do they feel present, or like
  furniture? Does Pello's deed dialog at depth 10 flow?
- The Life Tree start: unlocks on completing depth 1. Does the crown
  view make sense at first sight? Does tapping a leaf feel right?
- Combat and pace: swings, damage feedback, the leisurely pace —
  does it feel good on Windows, or clunky, slow, unfair, unclear?
- Anything that breaks, freezes, or looks wrong — screenshots of
  weird moments are gold.

KNOWN ISSUES (already diagnosed — no need to re-report, but tell us
if you see them, since confirmation still helps):
- Opening the Life Tree can let HUD elements (health, minimap, etc.)
  show through on top of it, and HUD buttons may stay tappable
  through the tree. Root cause found (layer order); fix is parked
  for a future build.
- The Life Tree button can sit clipped at the screen edge (a stale
  height number). Same story: diagnosed, fix parked.
