ABYSMAL — Monster Material List
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas


Every base creature from the roster, grouped by family. James fills in the Materials column. Format per line: Monster name | Family | Materials: 


RULES (locked):


* Variants (~1,000 with attunements / corruption / stature) inherit their base creature's materials unless noted otherwise.
* Light leather comes from small beasts; heavy leather from large beasts (bears and their weight class).
* Teeth / fangs are Undercrucible reagents: grind them up for damage + a status effect, or mount them on weapons directly. (Locked example: snake fangs, ground = damage + poison.)
* Only THREE rows are pre-filled as locked examples: wolf -> fur, boar -> leather, spider -> silk. Everything else is blank for James to fill in.
* Cross-listed creatures appear once, under their primary family, with their other family noted.
