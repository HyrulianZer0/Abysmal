ABYSMAL — Texture inspiration: the 5 missing themes
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas
Anchor (Nov 1): 1K quality, realistic, one house resource pack.
Source libraries: Poly Haven + ambientCG (both CC0, 1K available).
Status: INSPIRATION ONLY (Nov 1, 2026) — no textures downloaded,

## 1. Flooded Deep
Look: submerged stone, waterline staining, slick wet rock, puddle
reflections. Palette: deep teal / green-black, wet highlights.
Search terms (Poly Haven / ambientCG): water, wet stone, puddle,
mossy wet rock, underwater rock.
Relation to existing folders: swamp + mossy_dark are cousins —
Flooded Deep goes darker and WETTER (submerged, not damp).

## 2. Bone Wastes
Look: parched cracked earth, bleached dry sand, scattered remains.
Palette: bone-white, arid tan, dust grey.
Search terms: cracked earth, dry soil, sand, desert ground.
Honest gap: true bone-surface PBR textures are rare. The theme is
carried by cracked earth + sand surfaces; bone reads come from
props/models, not the floor texture.
Bonus find (models, not surfaces): Polyy.AI "3D Fossils, Bones &
Skulls Pack" (itch.io, CC0, 54 skulls/bones/fossils in GLB with PBR
textures + low-poly LOD set) — set dressing for the Wastes, if the
director wants it. Itch.io clickthrough rules apply.

## 3. Fungal Grotto
Look: damp cave walls + mushroom/moss colonies, soft glow accents.
Palette: deep green-brown rock, phosphorescent teal/green pops.
Search terms: moss, mushroom, cave rock, lichen, fungi.
Relation to existing folders: cavern + mossy_dark are the base —
Fungal Grotto adds the organic colony layer (growth ON rock).

## 4. Void Scar
Look: near-black fractured rock, faint violet/cyan veining,
starfield-dark negative space. Palette: black, midnight blue,
violet glints.
Search terms: obsidian, black rock, volcanic glass, dark stone.
Hardest theme to source as a PBR surface (void = absence). The
trick: dark fractured stone + emissive-leaning veins, letting
darkness do the work. Reference look: glossy black obsidian macro
shots (blue/purple iridescence) — but photos are NOT seamless PBR;
the pack's tiling treatment matters most here.

## 5. Storm Spire
Look: rain-slicked high stone, wind-scoured rock, lightning-lit
greys. Palette: slate grey, wet sheen, storm blue.
Search terms: wet stone, slate, rain stone, storm rock.
Relation to existing folders: stone + cavern are the base — Storm
Spire = stone + wet sheen + height. Weather reads as lighting
more than surface.

Every picked texture goes through the same pipeline: downscale to

uses NEAREST — the pack pass flips it). Credit to original devs
for resources AND inspiration, per the Nov 1 policy.
