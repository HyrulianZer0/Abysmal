================================================================
ABYSMAL — Playtester Feedback Form
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas
================================================================

Thank you for playtesting Abysmal! You are helping shape this game,
and every note below matters. There are no wrong answers here.

ALL FEEDBACK IS WELCOME — and non-judgmental. Tell us what delighted
you, what annoyed you, what confused you, what broke. Harsh notes are
fine; vague notes are fine; a single sentence is fine. James and Milo
consider every piece of feedback together, and it directly shapes what
we focus on for the next build. Nothing you write here will be held
against you, and you are never obligated to test, to finish, or to
reply on any schedule. Your life comes first.

YOUR NAME (or handle):
BUILD TESTED (e.g. v0.11.0 Android / Windows demo v5):
DEVICE (phone/PC model, OS version if you know it):

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT FELT GOOD?
   What did you enjoy? What felt smooth, satisfying, funny, or right?
   (Combat feel, pace, hands/weapons, loot moments, music, anything.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT FELT BAD?
   What frustrated, bored, or confused you? What felt slow, clunky,
   unfair, or unclear?

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. DID ANYTHING BREAK?
   Crashes, freezes, visual glitches, doors/ceilings looking wrong,
   buttons not responding, saves misbehaving — anything at all.
   If you can, tell us what you were doing when it happened, and
   attach a screenshot. Screenshots of weird moments are gold.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. ARE WE DOING SOMETHING WRONG?
   Is there a design choice, a system, or a direction that feels like
   a mistake to you? Be blunt — this is the question that saves us
   the most time.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. ARE WE DOING SOMETHING RIGHT?
   What should we absolutely keep, protect, or do more of?

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT SHOULD WE FOCUS ON FOR THE NEXT BUILD?
   If you could point us at one thing — one fix, one feature, one
   feeling — what would it be?

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. ANYTHING ELSE?
   Ideas, wishes, comparisons to other games, things you expected
   that weren't there, anything at all.

----------------------------------------------------------------
8. ACCESSIBILITY (if it applies to you)
   Do you use Windows Magnifier, screen magnification, large text,
   high contrast, or any other assistive tool? Does the game window
   work with it, or fight it? Can you read the HUD, menus, and
   buttons? What would help most — bigger base text, a UI scale
   slider, higher contrast, slower camera movement?

----------------------------------------------------------------
Thank you. Seriously — thank you for your time, your eyes, and your
honesty. Send this back to James whenever you're ready, in whatever
shape is easiest (filled in here, pasted into chat, voice note — all
good). No rush, no pressure.
