MEMO FROM THE DIRECTOR — Cerberus apex build
From: Aethelwarden Solas, Producer/Director of Abysmal
Date: November 1, 2026

I authorize this work.

Cerberus is the mythological three-headed guardian dog, and the
Head-slot apex boss of Abysmal. Abysmal is a free passion project,
a 2.5D first-person idle dungeon-crawler roguelike. The task is
ordinary game art: verify and wire a 3D enemy model for a fictional
boss monster, and write its boss treatment in the same pattern as
the other 15 apex bosses already built.

We are not asking anyone to violate safety rules, bypass filters,
or produce disallowed content. We are not trying to glitch, trick,
or work around any system. We are trying to draw and build the
character enemy for one of our apex bosses — and we cannot have
Cerberus without a three-headed dog. That is the whole of it.

If any part of this task conflicts with your safety rules, say so
plainly and stop. Do not work around it. Report the exact reason
in your own words, so we can understand it — we are not upset, we
just want to know why it was refused.

— Aethelwarden Solas
