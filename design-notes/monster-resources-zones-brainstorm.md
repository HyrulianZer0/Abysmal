# Monsters, Resources & Zones — Brainstorm Paper

**Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas**
**Status: BRAINSTORM ONLY — no code, no builds, no decisions.** Everything below is options for James to react to, piece by piece.

**Locked anchors this paper builds on (Oct 24):**
- Currency ladder: gold (mortal) → **Hope** (town → Life Tree) → **Ambrosia** (rebirth → Temple).
- The Life Tree is part of the player's soul and persists through rebirth; only the mortal life resets.
- **Monster resource drops are locked in principle:** wolf → fur, boar → leather, spider → silk. "Use the monster to the advantage of the player."
- **Gathering zones are BOTH** guild-dispatch destinations AND places the player can delve.
- Town buildings: built one at a time, player's choice, greyed out until requirements are met; each needs its **relevant resources** (not just gold) to build and level.
- Guild Hall = dispatch center; leveling it grows dispatch slots.
- Existing rules that still apply: drops are random; staged CC0 assets only, no AI-generated assets, primitives as fallback; the main abyss today is dark brick + moss.

## Workstream 1 — Monster → Resource Drops

**The idea in one line:** every monster family drops something thematic. More monsters = more materials = more reasons to hunt specific families.

**Drop tiers (brainstorm):** each family gets a **common** material (drops often) and a **rarer** one (drops seldom, worth hunting for). Exact rates are tuning, not design — and everything below the three locked examples is an option, flagged where it's shaky.

**Who consumes what (first sketch):**
- **Smith** ← metal, leather, scale, chitin, bone, hide
- **Alchemist** ← herbs, venom, blood, spores, roots, inks
- **Arcanum (enchanting)** ← silk, dust, feathers, cores, shards, essences
- **Town building** ← wood, stone, clay, cloth, furnishings

### Brainstorm notes (options, not rulings)

- **Silk tiers:** common spider silk → rarer Jorōgumo silk → rarest something (Tsuchigumo? boss-tier). Enchanting quality could scale with silk tier. Option.
- **Wood from monsters:** Leshy bark, cedar bark, dryad heartwood — monsters can feed the *building* economy too, not just crafting. Option.
- **Clay and stone from constructs:** golems and terracotta soldiers drop building material, which makes construct-heavy floors a quarry run. Option.
- **Attunement-flavored drops?** An Ember Wolf dropping charred pelt instead of fur, a Frost Wolf dropping frost-touched pelt — variant drops would multiply the material list fast. Tempting, but it could bloat the inventory. Flagged as an open question.
- **Human drops stay loot-like** (coin, lockpicks, notes) rather than body-part materials — keeps the tone right. Option.
- **Uncertain mappings (marked ? above):** Celtic, Egyptian, and goblin-kin drops are the shakiest — they need James's eye before they harden.

## Workstream 2 — Gathering Zone Themes

**The idea in one line:** three gentler dungeons that feed the town. Each has its own look, its own monsters, and its own yield — and each works two ways: send adventurers (dispatch) or delve it yourself.

### The Forest — wood

- **Look & feel:** canopy-filtered light, dappled green-gold, mossy trunks for walls, leaf litter underfoot, root tangles in the corridors. Warm and alive after the abyss's cold brick.
- **Textures:** bark walls, leaf-litter floor, fern and root props, shafts of light.
- **Zone monsters (from the roster):** Wolf, Wild Boar, Grizzly, Panther, Deep Stag, Leshy, Dryad, Walking Oak, Thornhound, Spriggan, Satyr, Faun, Pixie, Sprite, Bramble Imp.
- **Yields:** wood (common), heartwood (rare, from Leshy/Dryad/Walking Oak), fur and leather (from beasts), forest herbs (floor pickups).
- **Dispatch flavor:** "logging expedition" — adventurers return with timber and pelts.

### The Caverns — stone & metal

- **Look & feel:** rough-hewn granite, glinting ore veins in the walls, stalactites overhead, crystal glints in the dark. The closest cousin to the main abyss, but warmer — torchlight on worked stone instead of cold brick.
- **Textures:** granite walls with ore seams, gravel floor, crystal clusters, timber shoring props.
- **Zone monsters (from the roster):** Cave Rat, Cave Spider, Cave Scorpion, Kobold, Gnome, Earth Elemental, Stone Golem, Cave Bear, Burrow Worm, Blind Mole, Deep Crab, Cave Frog.
- **Yields:** stone (common), metal ore (common), gems (rare), clay (from golems/terracotta-adjacent floors).
- **Dispatch flavor:** "mining expedition" — adventurers return with stone and ore. (Harlan Slate's "Well Stocked" material-yield buff matters most here.)

### The Swamp — herbs

- **Look & feel:** black water underfoot, hanging moss, drifting mist, cypress knees, bioluminescent fungi. Quiet, eerie, beautiful — the prettiest danger in the game.
- **Textures:** murky water floor, reed walls, mist particles, glowing fungus props.
- **Zone monsters (from the roster):** Mire Pudding, Cave Frog, Crocodile, Rusalka, Vodyanoy, Kikimora, Will-o'-wisp, Vampire Vine, Fungal Shambler, Marsh Troll, Kappa, Nure-onna, Mandrake.
- **Yields:** herbs (common), rare blooms (Corpse Flower, Mandrake root), venom glands, swamp reed, peat.
- **Dispatch flavor:** "herb-gathering expedition" — adventurers return with potion ingredients.

### Cross-zone notes (options)

- **Zone depth scaling?** Gathering zones could scale gently with the player's depth (like the abyss does) or stay fixed-difficulty "safe" content. Open question.
- **Zone-exclusive monsters?** Some monsters could live ONLY in their zone (e.g., Kappa only in the Swamp), giving each zone a hunting identity — or everything could also appear in the abyss. Open question.
- **Dispatch vs delve yields:** should a personal delve yield more than a dispatch trip (you're there, you risk more), or the same? Open question.

**Brainstorm notes (options):**
- Themes could be **depth bands** (the abyss changes skin as you descend), **variant floors** (a theme floor appears randomly among brick floors), or **side areas** (like the gathering zones). All three are on the table.
- Cheapest implementation is palette + texture + monster-weight swaps on the existing generator — no new geometry system. Option.
- Thematic drops could tie in: a Frozen Vault floor's wolves drop frost-touched pelts, an Ember Foundry floor's golems drop magma hearts. Same attunement-drop question as Workstream 1.

## ANSWERS — James's decisions, Oct 29, 2026 (recorded by coordinator)

These answer the six open questions above. They are design decisions for
later waves, not new builds — nothing here is implemented yet.

1. **Drop mappings stand as filled — none felt wrong.** The filled
   materials list (`monster-material-list-filled-2026-10-29.txt`) remains
   the drops authority; no family needs better materials on this pass.
2. **Gathering zones scale with player depth FIRST, then ease up if the
   same thing kills you multiple times** — an adaptive mercy mechanic.
   Tuning decided Nov 1: each mercy trigger slashes the foe's HP by
   10% of its current value (compounding, so it never reaches zero),
   lasting until the player defeats that foe; attack untouched.
3. **Zone-exclusive monsters decided Nov 1: RANDOM, no exclusivity
   list.** Monsters are not coded per zone; placement stays random
   like themes. Rationale (James): the game's purpose isn't
   resource farming, it's the chance to enjoy a game with no
   endgame.
4. **BOTH base and attuned drops.** An attuned variant (e.g. charred
   pelt) is itself the instant elemental tell — the variant drop IS
   how the player reads the element at a glance.
5. **ALL nine dungeon themes excite him.** Structure decided Nov 1,
   2026: RANDOM — themes are assigned randomly, not by depth band,
   variant floor, or side area.
6. **Phoenix ash APPROVED as the rebirth reagent — NOT a feather.**
   (Square Enix's Phoenix Down owns the feather image.) Flavor line,
   in James's words: "the warmth from the ash warms your cold body as
   you rise back up to your feet."
