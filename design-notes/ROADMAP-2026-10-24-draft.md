WHAT CHANGED OCT 24 (worked through Jeff's feedback, piece by piece):
- Platinum is renamed HOPE. Town strength raises the people's hope;
  Hope is spent on the Life Tree. Currency ladder is now
  gold (mortal) -> Hope -> Ambrosia (divine).
- The persistent tree is the LIFE TREE (supersedes "Soul Tree").
  It IS the player's soul: it persists through rebirth, with all its
  permanent rewards. Hope builds upon / grows the tree.
- PRESTIGE, defined by James: building the town and receiving its
  buffs — PERMANENT buffs (soul-level, survive rebirth) and RUN-BASED
  buffs (this life only, re-earned each life). A new life gives up
  only the run-based buffs. This closes the Oct 17 prestige-as-action
  question.
- REBIRTH (not sacrifice) grants Ambrosia for ascension at the Temple.
  Only the player character resets (life / delve / gear); town, Life
  Tree, Hope and everyone else persist — and that persistence is what
  makes the rewards greater.
- Town buildings are built ONE AT A TIME, in PLAYER-CHOSEN order,
  greyed out until requirements are met. Each has LEVELS raising its
  stat bonuses and unlocking more options. Building requires its
  RELEVANT RESOURCES (gathering-zone materials, not just gold).
- Guild Hall is the dispatch centre; levelling it grows dispatch slots.
- Gathering zones are BOTH dispatch destinations AND personally
  delveable: Forest (wood), Caverns (stone/metal), Swamp (herbs).
  Monsters drop thematic resources (wolf -> fur, boar -> leather,
  spider -> silk); drops feed crafting/building.
- The Arcanum is the MAGE'S GUILD (spells + enchanting). Adventurers
  auto-cast their spells; the player levels it early for adventurer
  spells, player enchantments come along the way, and a Guild Master
  is required for a specific task/floor later.
- Town is next, at depth 10, via Pello's deed scene.

LIFE TREE NAVIGATION — LAURELS, not rings (final correction, Oct 24):

  -> that founder's stats panel + the pages of related buffs.
