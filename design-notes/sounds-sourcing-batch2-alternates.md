## License standard used here (same as batch 1)

- VERIFIED means the license text was seen ON the source page itself in
  this pass (page fetch or the page's own text via search). A
  third-party list claiming CC0 is NOT enough — third-party claims are
  labeled as such and never upgraded to verified.
- CC0 preferred; CC-BY acceptable with credit. Custom free licenses
  (Pixabay Content License, Mixkit SFX Free License, itch.io free
  commercial, freeSFX credit license) are acceptable for IN-GAME EMBED
  ONLY, never standalone redistribution — same rule as batch 1.
- NOT shippable, unchanged: SFXMint (terms unresolved/conflicting),
  SoundFactory, Violet Recording (prototyping-only per ToS), BBC, Meta
  AudioCraft, Zapsplat-free. Account-gated sources are out for this
  batch by definition (Freesound, Uppbeat). Paid sources are out
  (Soundstripe, asoundeffect, Sonniss bundles — GB-scale, deprioritized
  in batch 1). CC-BY-SA and GFDL files (common on Wikimedia Commons)
  are NOT in the approved license list and are not recommended here.
- A pitched variation of a generic sound is NOT a true vocal, and a
  trimmed ambience is NOT a creature vocal. Both are labeled as such
  wherever they appear below.

  "All sound clips are royalty-free and can be used without
  attribution, plus no sign up is required" and "you can use
  these sounds in both commercial and personal projects";
  Sonilo's direct license read (Sept 2026) confirms the SFX
  license "covers uses including video games, TV and radio
  broadcasts, CDs and DVDs." NOTE: Mixkit's MUSIC license is
  different (no video games) — do not confuse the two; only the
  SOUND EFFECTS license is cited here.
  https://mixkit.co/license/ · https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- freeSFX terms (on-page, freesfx.co.uk/sfx/frogs): free for
  commercial/non-commercial/broadcast production including games
  and apps; credit freesfx.co.uk with URL required; no isolated
  use (must be mixed into other audio/visual elements — satisfied
  by in-game use); cannot claim as own or redistribute to others.

  attribution required (appreciated); no reselling/redistributing
  the assets as a pack.
- Pixabay Content License framework: per batch 1 (free commercial
  use, no attribution, modification allowed, no standalone
  redistribution; pre-2019 uploads CC0). Individual Pixabay pages
