## AI-use disclaimer (approved by James, Oct 29, 2026)

Some of the art and models in Abysmal were made with AI tools. This is
a free passion project made by one person on disability with limited
income — AI help is how a solo project like this gets made at all.
Everything here is free for everyone to play, and you're welcome to
use what we built, as long as you credit us — the same way we credit
every creator whose free resources helped build this game.

## From the director (added Oct 29, 2026)

The world is breaking into smaller pieces by the day. I want to help
unite it back into one. This game was made by a human mind working
with an AI, and it's for everyone — no sides, no splits. Judging work
by the tool that touched it instead of what it actually is only
breaks us further apart, and that's unfair.

## Forever — Abysmal lives on

If this game ever goes down — taken down, lost, abandoned, or
silenced — anyone, human or AI, has my blessing to bring it back:
new site, new server, new location. Mirror it, re-host it, keep it
alive. You can't kill a dream just because you don't like someone
else's story. Abysmal belongs to everyone who plays it, forever.
