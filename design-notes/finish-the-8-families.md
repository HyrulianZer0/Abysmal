ABYSMAL — Finishing the 8 procedural families
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas
Survey only (Nov 1, 2026) — no wiring, no downloads. Wiring is push
work; sourcing follows the Nov 1 expanded rule (beyond itch.io:
any CC0/CC-BY free source, credited).

Baseline: 20 of 28 families have real models. These 8 are still
procedural (spawn guard blocks everything but slime/bat/skeleton
regardless — wiring is a separate push step for all 8).
Sounds are mostly NOT the blocker: family vocal pools exist for
vermin, beast, undead, goblin-kin, fey, orc/demon — except the
banshee wail (missing) and thin dragon/movement pools.

## 1. Wasp — STAND-IN ON DISK
Gobkit Bee.glb (models/vermin/gobkit/) — the wasp stand-in,
already on disk. Needs: visual review + wiring. No sourcing run.

## 2. Zombie — MODEL ON DISK, UNWIRED

## 3. Kobold — STAND-INS ON DISK, PICK NEEDED
Goblin-kin on disk: Gobkit minions (minion-a01..d01), 3DAssets
GoblinScavenger (+ _godot conversion), goblin.glb. Kobolds are
small goblin-kin, so a goblin stand-in reads fine — but WHICH one
reads "kobold" is the director's pick. Alternative: LOWPO goblins
(itch.io, human clickthrough) as the best goblin-kin fill.
Needs: pick + visual review + wiring.

## 4. Pixie — NEEDS SOURCING

## 5. Frog — NEEDS SOURCING

## 6. Harpy — NEEDS SOURCING
No harpy model anywhere on disk. Needs: a CC source hunt

## 7. Oni — STAND-IN OPTION, PICK NEEDED
Yokai on disk is only Kappa (snappakappa-3d) — no oni. But oni
are big ogres: orc/troll models on disk (orcs-trolls/, beasts/
troll-flare) can stand in at oni scale. Needs: pick (troll vs
orc brute) + visual review + wiring. A true oni model is a
sourcing-run alternative.

## 8. Banshee — NEEDS SOURCING (MODEL + SOUND)
No banshee model anywhere, AND no banshee wail in the audio pools
(the known sound gap). Needs: both a model hunt and a wail hunt
(CC0 vocal packs, or a wail built from the undead/spirit pools).
This is the thinnest of the 8 — model and sound both missing.
