ABYSMAL — Apex Uniques: Suggestions
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas

STATUS: ACCEPTED by James, Oct 29, 2026. The 16 items below are the
apex unique set. Effect numbers remain provisional and must be tuned
against the locked balance curves, not set by this doc.

1. CERBERUS — Head
   Item: Gatekeeper's Triune Helm
   Effect: Ambush first-strikes lose their free hit (mimic bites,
   trap lunges, anything that strikes before you can act). Small DEF.
   Why: Three heads watch every direction; the gate lets none out
   unannounced. The helm means nothing ever catches your back turned.

2. MEDUSA — Face
   Item: Visage of the Gorgon (mask)
   Effect: Your crits have a chance to petrify — the target slows,
   then is briefly stone-held.
   Why: Her gaze is the weapon, and the face slot is where a gaze
   lives. You wear the monster's curse and turn it outward.

3. ECHIDNA — Neck
   Item: Torc of the Broodmother
   Effect: Bonus monster-material yield — the Mother's children
   bring you tribute.
   Why: She never hunts alone; her brood does it for her. The torc
   makes every brood you meet pay a tithe in materials.

4. FAFNIR — Shoulders
   Item: Pauldrons of the Hoard
   Effect: Bonus gold from kills and loot. Small poison resistance.
   Why: He murdered for cursed gold and slept on it. Greed, worn on
   the shoulders, pays you back. The poison resist nods to his breath.

5. TYPHON — Chest
   Item: Stormplate of the Hundred Heads
   Effect: You cannot be knocked back, and melee attackers take
   storm damage in return.
   Why: The storm-giant the gods fled. His plate makes you the
   mountain the wind breaks against, and the storm answers back.

6. THE TARASQUE — Back (cape/cloak)
   Item: Shellcloak of the River
   Effect: Bonus DEF, and damage from frontal attacks is reduced.
   Why: The shell that shrugged off knights' swords. Worn as a cape,
   it turns your front into a wall — the way Martha tamed it, not
   with a sword, but the shell still does the work.

7. HUMBABA — Arms
   Item: Cedar Vambraces of the Warden
   Effect: Your melee hits send a small shockwave, splashing damage
   to adjacent enemies.
   Why: Trunk-sized arms that smashed the ground. The vambraces put
   his Cedar Slam in your fists.

8. FENRIR — Hands
   Item: Gauntlets of Gleipnir's Bite
   Effect: Bonus crit damage (feeds the crit-overflow rule), and your
   crits briefly stagger the target.
   Why: The bite that took Tyr's hand, bound by an impossible chain.
   Hands are where his story lives — crushing bite, binding chain.

9. JORMUNGANDR — Waist
   Item: Ouroboros Belt of the World Serpent
   Effect: Slow HP regen, and poison damage is reduced to a trickle.
   Why: Tail in mouth, endless renewal. Thor died of the serpent's
   venom nine steps later — the belt makes sure venom never finishes
   you.

10. YAMATA NO OROCHI — Legs
    Item: Greaves of the Eightfold Scale
    Effect: Bonus move speed, and slowing ground effects (venom pools,
    snares, mire) cannot slow you.
    Why: Eight heads, eight tails, sure footing through any storm.
    The greaves carry the serpent's scale — not Kusanagi, which stays
    in Orochi's lore, per the earlier mapping note.

11. ANZU — Feet
    Item: Talon Boots of the Tablet Thief
    Effect: Bonus move speed and lightning resistance; your steps
    carry a whisper of the storm.
    Why: The storm-bird himself was the storm's accomplice. Feet are
    for the dive, the gust, the getaway — the boots keep you ahead
    of the lightning you used to flee.

12. KOSCHEI THE DEATHLESS — Ring (left)
    Item: Needle Ring of the Deathless
    Effect: Once per life, a lethal hit leaves you at 1 HP instead.
    The ring then sleeps until your next life.
    Why: His death was never in him — needle, egg, duck, hare, chest.
    The ring hides a sliver of your death somewhere else, so the
    killing blow finds nothing to take.

13. RAVANA — Ring (right)
    Item: Signet of Ten Crowns
    Effect: Your attacks have a chance to strike twice — a phantom
    echo mirrors your blow.
    Why: Ten heads, and illusion doubles that mirror his attacks.
    The signet lends you one of his heads: a second strike from
    nowhere.

14. BABA YAGA — Trinket
    Item: Hut Charm on Chicken Legs
    Effect: Bonus loot rarity/quality, and a small chance a defeated
    enemy drops a bonus "gift" roll.
    Why: Answer her questions right and you leave with a gift; rude
    guests become dinner. The charm is the polite answer, carried
    with you — the forest's gifts keep finding you.

15. SURTR — Weapon
    Item: Dawnblade of Muspelheim
    Effect: Every swing deals bonus fire damage; crits erupt in a
    small burst of flame around the target.
    Why: The sword brighter than the sun, lord of the world of fire.
    The weapon slot was always his — the blade that ends worlds,
    sized for one hand.

16. SHUTEN-DOJI — Off-hand
    Item: Gourd of the Drunken King
    Effect: Sip from the gourd to heal and gain a brief attack and
    speed surge; the longer a fight lasts, the stronger the frenzy
    grows.
    Why: He was beaten the clever way — drunk on poisoned sake, struck
    while he dreamed. The gourd turns his weakness into your ritual:
    drink, surge, and end it before the frenzy ends you.

OPEN QUESTIONS (James's call):
SET BONUSES — ACCEPTED by James, Oct 29, 2026. Tiers are cumulative:
wearing 8 pieces grants the 4- and 8-piece bonuses, and so on.
Numbers provisional; tune against locked curves.
- 4 pieces — "Monster's Regard": bonus max HP. The relics steady
  your body; the dungeon's weight sits easier on a warden-in-making.
- 8 pieces — "Apex Authority": non-apex monsters deal reduced damage
  to you. Lesser denizens recognize what you wear and pull their blows.
- 12 pieces — "Warden's Tithe": town buildings produce bonus Hope.
  The Abyss pays tribute to its warden, feeding the Life Tree sink.
- 16 pieces — CAPSTONE: "Crown of the Deep" — bonus damage to all
  monsters, apex-tier included, and they deal reduced damage to you.
  The Crown is the conqueror's crown, perfect for one who defeated
  all 16 apexes. Whether the crown serves good or evil is not a
  capstone choice — it is shown through the player's actions. There
  is no endgame in which the world or universe can be destroyed, so
  the stakes are personal, not cosmic.
- No-set-bonus option declined by James Oct 29 (he loves the ladder).
- Revisions: James approved all 16 as written Oct 29; any future
  change is his call. Warden of the Abyss capstone removed Oct 29
  per James; Crown of the Deep is the sole capstone.
- Effect numbers: all provisional. Tune against the locked curves
  when wiring, never from this doc alone.
