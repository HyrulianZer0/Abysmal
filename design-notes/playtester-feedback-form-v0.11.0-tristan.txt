================================================================
ABYSMAL — Playtester Feedback Form, v0.11.0 (Tristan edition)
Producer/Director: Aethelwarden Solas
================================================================

Thank you for playing! This form is for the Windows demo of v0.11.0
(the same build Jeff and Fen tested). Answer what you can, skip what
you can't. Plain text on purpose — it works with screen magnifiers
and text scaling. Send your answers back to James however is easiest
for you; he passes them to the dev table.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ACCESSIBILITY — Windows Magnifier (the most important part)
----------------------------------------------------------------
This build was made BEFORE the accessibility pass, so please report
everything that breaks. The goal is full Windows Magnifier
compatibility, plus large text and UI scale later.

1. Does the game window work with Windows Magnifier at all?
   (Windowed? Fullscreen? Does Magnifier follow the game, or does
   the game fight it?)

2. Can you read the HUD with Magnifier — health, gold, depth, the
   log panel, button labels?

3. Can you read the menus — Settings, town management (clipboard),
   the Life Tree / laurel leaves and their stats?

4. Is any text too small, too low-contrast, or cut off at the
   screen edge, even with Magnifier?

5. Does anything flicker, jump, or move too fast to follow with
   Magnifier on?

6. What would help most: bigger base text, a UI scale slider,
   higher contrast, slower camera movement, something else?

----------------------------------------------------------------
GAMEPLAY FEEL
----------------------------------------------------------------
7. Delve pace (leisurely / normal / swift): does the dungeon read
   clearly at your pace? Too fast, too slow?

8. Combat readability: can you tell what is happening — who hit
   whom, how hard, when you are in danger?

9. The hand and weapon: visible enough? Can you tell slash from
   jab, and when a chest is in reach?

10. Chests and mimics: can you tell a normal chest from a mimic
    before opening? (Mimics breathe faintly; the rich, ornate ones
    are the rare mini-boss kind.)

11. Death and rebirth: is it clear what happened and what you kept?

----------------------------------------------------------------
PERFORMANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------
12. Frame rate and stutters: smooth, or does it hitch anywhere
    (big rooms, lots of monsters, new floors)?

13. Any missing textures, black voids, or things that look wrong?

14. Your PC (roughly — Windows version, laptop or desktop, any
    graphics card if you know it):

----------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN COMMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Anything else — what you liked, what frustrated you, what you
wish the game did. There are no wrong answers here.

Thank you — your eyes shape what comes next.
